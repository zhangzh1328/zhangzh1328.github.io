About Me

I am an incoming PhD student at the University of Macau. My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, bioinformatics, and computational medicine.

I develop graph-based, multimodal, and self-supervised learning methods for single-cell and spatial multi-omics data, with a focus on spatial domain identification, cell-state characterisation, cross-sample integration, and biologically informed inference. I also work with clinical and longitudinal health data for prognosis, patient representation learning, and precision medicine.

Research Interests

  • Single-cell & spatial multi-omics. Spatial domain identification, cell states, and integration across samples and modalities.
  • Graph & self-supervised learning. Graph representation learning, contrastive learning, and multimodal learning.
  • Computational medicine. Electronic health records, longitudinal patient modelling, and outcome prediction.
