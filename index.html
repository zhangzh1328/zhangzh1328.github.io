About Me

Incoming PhD student at the University of Macau researching AI-driven bioinformatics and computational medicine. I develop graph-based, multimodal, and self-supervised learning methods to analyze single-cell/spatial multi-omics and longitudinal clinical data, aiming to advance cellular characterization, patient representation learning, and precision medicine.

Research Interests

  • Single-Cell & Spatial Multi-Omics: Characterizing tissue architecture, interpreting cellular heterogeneity, and developing cross-modal data integration.
  • Machine Learning & Representation Learning: Graph neural networks, self-supervised learning, and multimodal learning.
  • Computational Medicine: Longitudinal electronic health record, patient similarity analysis, and clinical outcome prediction.
